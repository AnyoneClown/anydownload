How your connection and images are handled

Tests, album requests and uploads run on this device. Connect to your server’s local network or Tailscale network before uploading. HTTP and HTTPS are supported; an Immich /albums address is normalized to its server.

Your account stores the encrypted key and connection settings. Images travel directly from Firefox to Immich.
