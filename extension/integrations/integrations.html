Connect a server

HTTP, HTTPS, LAN addresses and Tailscale hostnames are supported. An Immich /albums address is normalized to its server.

Create a key in Immich with asset.upload. To choose albums, also grant album.read, albumAsset.create and user.read. Keys are encrypted in your AnyDownload account and are never shown again.

Disconnecting deletes the saved key and connection; uploaded images stay in Immich.
